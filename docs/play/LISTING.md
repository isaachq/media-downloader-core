### Full Description
```
Media Download Core is a high-performance batch downloader engineered for reliable, offline media archiving with built-in cryptographic file verification.

Built on an embedded Node.js core, the app processes individual links, profiles, and extensive playlists using a dedicated job queue designed to operate efficiently without draining battery or causing thermal throttling.

KEY FEATURES:

• Heavy-Duty Batch Processing: Queue individual posts, video reels, or entire creator profiles. Choose video (MP4) or audio-only formats with automatic quality selection.
• Integrity Verification (File by File): Every completed download undergoes immediate integrity validation to ensure frames decode cleanly and files are not corrupted.
• Persistent Foreground Service: Keep long-running batch downloads active even with your phone locked or the screen turned off. Dynamic notifications display real-time progress, completed items, percentages, and estimated time remaining.
• Gallery Integration: Automatically publish finished media to your device's shared Movies, Music, or Pictures collections (MediaStore) without demanding invasive storage permissions.
• Privacy-First Architecture: no ads, no advertising trackers, no advertising ID. Optional anonymous usage statistics (which features are used and what fails, never links, titles or files) that you can turn off in Settings.
• Local Authentication: Optional site sign-in takes place via an embedded sandbox browser. Your session cookies never leave your device and are never sent to external servers or relays.
• Clean Material 3 Design: Full dynamic theme support (Light, Dark, and System modes) and adaptive themed monochrome launcher icons for Android 13+.

RESPONSIBLE USE & NON-AFFILIATION:
Media Download Core is an independent open-source utility designed for personal backup, offline study, and archiving of content you own or have explicit creator permission to download. The app is not affiliated with, authorized, or endorsed by TikTok, ByteDance, Instagram, Facebook, or Meta Platforms. Please respect local copyright laws and creator intellectual property rights.

This app doesn't support YouTube. Downloading from YouTube isn't allowed in the app, and YouTube links are refused with that message.
```

Impulsada por un motor Node.js nativo integrado, la aplicación administra descargas individuales, perfiles y listas extensas mediante una cola de trabajos optimizada para evitar sobrecalentamiento y ahorrar batería.

CARACTERÍSTICAS PRINCIPALES:

• Procesamiento por Lotes: Programa publicaciones individuales, reels o perfiles completos. Elige formato de video (MP4) o audio con selección automática de calidad óptima.
• Verificación de Integridad: Cada archivo descargado se valida de inmediato para garantizar que los fotogramas se decodifiquen correctamente y no haya archivos dañados.
• Servicio en Primer Plano Persistente: Mantén las descargas activas en segundo plano incluso con la pantalla apagada o el dispositivo bloqueado. Notificaciones continuas informan sobre el progreso, porcentaje completado y tiempo restante (ETA).
